# Source and verification policy

## Exam facts

Prefer the organizing body's official notice, edital, candidate portal, or official calendar. Record the source and retrieval date. Distinguish confirmed facts from assumptions and scenario values.

## Travel inventory

Use Decolar or Skyscanner when connected for flight discovery and comparison. Use Booking.com when connected for lodging discovery. Verify decision-critical details in the actual offer or provider page when possible. A connector result, search snippet, cached fare, or unattributed summary is not final confirmation.

For each candidate, capture:

- provider and source;
- retrieval timestamp and currency;
- full itinerary and terminal or airport changes;
- baggage included;
- taxes and unavoidable fees;
- cancellation or change restrictions that affect the decision;
- whether the result is a quote, a booking handoff, or a confirmed purchasable offer.

Search round trips and separate one-way tickets when useful. Check adjacent dates and alternate airports only when the complete door-to-door result can improve.

For Brazilian intercity buses and urban transport, use direct operators or reputable aggregators when no suitable connected tool exists. Cross-check schedules whose failure would threaten the exam.

## Lodging

Verify the actual check-in date needed. An arrival after midnight may require booking the preceding night and notifying the property of late arrival. Include taxes, cancellation terms, location, safety considerations, and transport to the exam venue.

## Actions

Research and comparison do not authorize booking, purchasing, messaging providers, changing calendars, saving to Drive, or starting recurring monitoring. Obtain explicit authorization immediately before each external action.

## Connector failures

If an app is unavailable, not authenticated, or incomplete, continue with authoritative public web sources and say which fallback was used. Never fabricate inventory, availability, fare rules, calendar entries, or email contents.
